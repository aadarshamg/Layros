# Collection banner artwork

Generated with the built-in image generation tool on October 20, 2026.
These images illustrate categories, not specific listed products.

Prompt brief: photorealistic minimal luxury fragrance category banner, landscape
3:2, warm ivory plaster and pale travertine, soft daylight from the left, restrained
forest-green fabric at far right. Subject group center-right, fully visible with
generous margins and empty ivory space at left. Natural textures and glass
reflections. No text, branding, labels, borders, people or clutter.

- `attar-v1.webp`: two small concentrated oil bottles with green/brass caps,
  pale amber oil, oud chip and jasmine.
- `gifts-v1.webp`: open ivory rigid gift box, three green-capped fragrance
  bottles, dark green ribbon and small jasmine blossom.
- `car-v1.webp`: hanging glass car-fragrance bottle, natural wooden cap,
  green cord, subtle blurred vehicle interior at the far right.

Optimized as 1200px-wide WebP files. Perfume, Shop All, gender, scent-profile
and New Launch banners reuse `../all-perfumes-background.png`; candle banners
reuse `../candle-collections/soy-v2.webp`.
